01About Akash

A lifetime at the piano, shared one lesson at a time

The piano has been at the centre of my life since childhood. Today I teach children, teenagers and adults, from complete beginners to students preparing for their final grade.

Having worked through the graded exams of both major international boards myself, I know how each one assesses, and I help every student choose the path that suits them.

My own diploma preparation shapes how I teach: technique always serves the music, and every piece is learned as if it will be performed.

Qualifications

  • Grade 8 PianoTrinity College London & ABRSM
  • ATCL Diploma in Piano PerformanceTrinity College London · Candidate

03How lessons work

From first message to first performance

Here’s what learning with me looks like, whether you’re five or fifty.

  1. 1

    Trial class

    We start with one lesson, at the studio or online. I listen to where you are and what you’d love to play.

  2. 2

    Your plan

    Together we pick a path and set a goal: a song to learn, an exam to take, or a performance to prepare.

  3. 3

    Weekly lessons

    One-to-one lessons every week, so you always leave knowing exactly what to practise next.

  4. 4

    Milestones

    Progress you can hear: your first full piece, an exam certificate, or your first time playing for an audience.

04Classes

Choose your path

All lessons are one-to-one, at my home piano studio or online, for students in India and overseas.

Play for joy

The music you love, at your own pace.

Who it’s for

Children and adults who want to play without exam pressure.

What you’ll learn

Film songs, pop songs and classical favourites, with reading, rhythm and chords built in.

Where it leads

Playing confidently for yourself, your family and your friends.

Exam path

Structured progress with recognised results.

Who it’s for

Students who want a qualification and steady, step-by-step progress.

What you’ll learn

Trinity or ABRSM pieces, scales, sight-reading, aural tests and music theory, with mock exams before the real one.

Where it leads

Graded certificates from Initial all the way to Grade 8.

Performance

From the practice room to the stage.

Who it’s for

Intermediate and advanced students ready to play for an audience.

What you’ll learn

Concert repertoire, interpretation, stage presence and how to handle nerves.

Where it leads

Confident performances at concerts and events.

Children can start from age 5, and adults are welcome at any level. Paths can be combined: many students prepare for exams while learning the songs they love.
